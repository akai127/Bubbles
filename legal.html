Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

The short version: this website is a digital sign. It has no forms, no accounts, no newsletter, no shopping cart, and no analytics. We do not ask you for any personal information, and we do not collect, store, or sell any.

Who we are

Bubbles & Sun is a laundromat and tanning salon at 279 Main St, Hudson Falls, NY 12839. This policy covers the website at bubbles-and-sun.com only. It does not cover what happens when you visit the shop in person, call us, or message us on Facebook.

Information we collect on this site

None that identifies you. There is no contact form, no login, no account, no order system, and no mailing-list signup anywhere on this website. We set no cookies of our own and we run no analytics or advertising software. We do not know who visits this site.

Third-party services this page loads

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and two things on it are served by Google. Each of those companies receives your IP address and basic browser information when the relevant part of the page loads, because that is how the internet delivers a file. We never see that data — it goes to them, not to us.

  • GitHub Pages (hosting). GitHub serves the page and keeps standard server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts. The two typefaces on this site are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.
  • Google Maps. The map on the main page is an embedded Google Map. Google may set cookies in that embed. If you would rather not load it, you can use the “Get directions” link instead, which does nothing until you click it. See Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

We set none. The embedded Google Map may set its own, as described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; nothing on this site will stop working if you do, apart from the map embed.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Separately, note that New York State law prohibits indoor tanning for anyone under 17, and 17-year-olds require written parental consent in person at the salon.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show you, correct, or delete. If you have contacted the shop directly — by phone, by e-mail, or through Facebook — and you want that conversation deleted, call us and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a booking form, an online payment option, or analytics to this site, we will update this page before that feature goes live and change the date at the top.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Bubbles & Sun wants every customer to be able to use this website, including people who browse with a screen reader, a keyboard only, magnification, or high-contrast settings.

Standard we aim for

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. As of the date above, this site has been checked against that standard and:

  • All text and interface colours meet the 4.5:1 contrast minimum (3:1 for large display text), verified by calculation rather than by eye.
  • Every part of the page can be reached and operated with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring, and a “Skip to main content” link is the first tab stop.
  • Headings run in order, the page declares its language, links describe where they go, and decorative artwork is hidden from screen readers.
  • The floating-bubble animation stops automatically for visitors whose device is set to “reduce motion.”
  • The layout reflows to a single column on small screens and survives zooming to 200%.

Known limitation

The store-location map is an embedded Google Map. We do not control its markup or its keyboard behaviour. The full street address and a plain “Get directions” link are given next to it in ordinary text so the map is never the only way to get that information.

Our physical store

This statement covers the website. If you need to know about access to the building itself at 279 Main St — parking, doorways, or help carrying laundry — please call us before you come and we will tell you exactly what to expect and what we can do to help.

Tell us if something does not work

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, we want to hear about it and we will fix it. Call (518) 747-7779 or stop in at 279 Main St, Hudson Falls, NY 12839. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will get back to you within five business days with either a fix or a date for one — and in the meantime we will give you the same information over the phone.
